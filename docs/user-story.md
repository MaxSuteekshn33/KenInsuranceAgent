# Part 1, Q1 — The story (≤100 words)

On 20 October 2026, Priya opens Ken to check her father Ramesh's health policy, renewing
in nine days. Ken flags it: his ₹5L cover caps room rent at 1% of sum insured, meaning a
real hospital stay could cost him lakhs out of pocket. Priya says, "can we fix this
without changing insurers." Ken drafts an email to the insurer asking to remove the cap
for a ₹1,200 premium increase, shows her the exact draft, and waits. She taps Approve.
Two days later the insurer agrees. Ken shows the new terms, Priya approves payment via
Pine Labs, and Ken couriers the updated policy document to their home via Delhivery.

(95 words)
